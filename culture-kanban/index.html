The rules
  • On your turn, choose a task that is not done, then draw a card.
  • Smooth hour (18): move the chosen task one step.
  • Breakthrough (2): move the chosen task straight to Done – or leave it and lift an external block.
  • Dependency (10): the chosen task turns out to need another task first (picked at random). It cannot move until that task is done.
  • External block (2): the chosen task moves to Blocked. It waits for an email, a decision or a delivery from outside the team.
  • Round 2 adds one rule: a WIP limit on In Progress for the whole team. A task that another task is waiting for may always be started, and does not count toward the limit.

In the simulation the players choose at random among the moves the rules allow, in both rounds. Any difference between the rounds comes from the WIP limit alone.
